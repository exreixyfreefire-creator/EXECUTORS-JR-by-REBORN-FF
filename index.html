| Who We Are

You are likely familiar with the name EXECUTORS. We currently operate two primary guilds: EXECUTORS SR and EXECUTORS JR. EX SR is led by the widely recognized EX JANIYA, while EX JR is led by EX KENZO. We successfully reunited our guild in June 2026. Notably, EXECUTORS JR currently holds the impressive rank of 12th in all of Sri Lanka for Guild Wars. If you are passionate about gaming and eager to become a part of our growing legacy, please get in touch with us at your earliest convenience to join our ranks.
